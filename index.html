Fugue State

A fugue that never ends. Each subject is written fresh and worked through exposition, episodes, inversion, augmentation and stretto, then dissolves into the next fugue without a pause.

Devices

Key, subject, voices, form and devices apply when the next fugue begins; “Begin a new fugue” brings it in sooner. Texture is live and reaches the music about three bars later, because the score is written that far ahead.
